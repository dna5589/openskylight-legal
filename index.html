OpenSkylight Calendar

OpenSkylight Calendar is a private family calendar display app that runs on a single wall-mounted computer in one household. It shows the family's events, chores, lists and meals on a touchscreen.

It can optionally connect to the owner's own Google Calendar so that events appear on the display and new events made on the display appear in Google Calendar. The app is not offered to the public and is used only by its developer's household.

The app is a personal fork of the open-source project lowerygt/OpenSkyLight.
